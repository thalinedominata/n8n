# Life Domains

The twenty domains of human life Go-Bot improves. The public-facing grid
renders from `src/data/life-domains.ts`, and each domain has a placeholder
page at `/domains/[id]` (immersive experiences arrive in Version 2).

Health · Education · Safety · Home · Children · Parents & Elders ·
Business · Finance · Mobility · Travel · Companionship · Fitness ·
Mental Health · Emergency · Faith · Entertainment · Environment · Pets ·
Accessibility · Developer Platform.

Domain deep-dives (scenarios, feature mapping, research) land here as
`kebab-case.md` files per domain in later versions. Adding a domain = one
entry in the data file; the grid, its page, and tests follow automatically.
