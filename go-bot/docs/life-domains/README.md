# Life Domains

The six domains of human life Go-Bot improves. The public-facing summary
renders from `src/data/life-domains.ts`.

Health & Wellbeing · Home & Daily Living · Learning & Growth ·
Work & Productivity · Play & Creativity · Connection & Care.

Domain deep-dives (scenarios, feature mapping, research) land here as
`kebab-case.md` files per domain in later versions.
